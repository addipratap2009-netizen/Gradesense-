HACK DEVENGERS 2.0 · SOLO SUBMISSION

Grade in seconds, not hours.

GradeSense — an AI-powered grading assistant that turns a photo of a handwritten answer sheet into a structured, rubric-based score with real feedback.

Built by Aditya · September 2026

THE PROBLEM

Grading eats hours it shouldn't.

Teachers grade 50–150 handwritten sheets per exam by hand — reading, comparing to the expected answer, scoring, and rarely finding time to explain why.

The result: slow turnaround, inconsistent marks, and students left with a number instead of feedback that helps them improve.

THE SOLUTION

Photo in, graded answer out.

What the teacher does

Uploads a photo of the answer sheet and the rubric or model answer for that question.

What GradeSense does

Reads the handwriting, checks it against the rubric concept-by-concept, and returns a score with specific per-question feedback.

HOW IT WORKS

Four steps, seconds not hours.

1

Upload the answer sheet

Photo of the handwritten response + the rubric/model answer.

2

OCR reads the handwriting

Text is extracted, with low-confidence words flagged.

3

Semantic grading

Each rubric concept is checked for meaning, not exact wording.

4

Teacher reviews & finalizes

Score and feedback are proposed — the teacher stays in control.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

A grading assistant, not a black box.

Semantic, not keyword

Recognizes a correct answer even when phrased differently from the model answer.

Transparent feedback

Explains why marks were given or deducted — not just a final number.

Human-in-the-loop

The AI proposes; the teacher always reviews and can override.

Confidence flags

Unclear handwriting is flagged for manual review, never silently guessed.
